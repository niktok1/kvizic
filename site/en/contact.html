When to write to us

  • requests about personal data: access, correction, erasure, objection (with your account ID, on the game's About screen, under Data);
  • deleting your account without the app (how);
  • a question with a wrong answer, if you cannot report it in the game;
  • an offensive player or room name;
  • bugs and suggestions.

We answer requests about personal data within 30 days at most.

Supervisory authority

If you think we process your data against the law, you can complain to Serbia's Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection: www.poverenik.rs. If you live outside Serbia, you can also turn to your country's supervisory authority.
