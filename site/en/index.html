Kvizić

A multiplayer quiz. The same questions, the same clock: whoever knows more, and faster, wins.

How it plays

  • Play right away. No sign-up: open the game and you play as a guest.
  • Make a room or join one. Invite friends with a six-digit code, join a public room, or tap Quick play.
  • Everyone answers at once. The faster you answer right, the more points; once you lock in, you see what the others picked.
  • The host decides. The number of questions, the time, the topics, the difficulty, the seats and whether a wrong answer costs.
  • Plenty of topics. Geography, history, sport, music, film, science, language, food, nature, art and more.
  • Or alone. Solo play, with your best score at each difficulty.

Where to play

On Android, through Google Play. Versions for iPhone and the browser come later. The questions are in Serbian.

The game is for people aged 13 and over.

No ads

The game has no ads and no purchases, and we never sell your data. What exactly we process and why is in the privacy policy.
